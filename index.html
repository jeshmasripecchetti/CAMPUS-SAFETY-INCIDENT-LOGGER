Campus Safety Incident Logger


Campus Safety Incident Logger is a web-based application designed to provide students, faculty members, and administrators with a secure and easy way to report campus safety incidents. The system enables users to report incidents, upload evidence, monitor complaint status, and help the administration respond quickly to emergencies.


Project Objectives

  • Improve campus security.
  • Allow online incident reporting.
  • Track complaints.
  • Generate reports and analytics.
  • Provide quick emergency response.

Main Features

  • Online Complaint Registration
  • Incident Tracking
  • Photo Upload
  • Admin Dashboard
  • Data Analytics
  • Secure Database Storage
