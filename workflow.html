Workflow Description


The Campus Safety Incident Logger provides a simple and secure process for reporting campus incidents. Students or staff log in, submit incident details, and upload supporting evidence such as photos. The information is securely stored and reviewed by the administrator. After verification, appropriate action is taken, and the dashboard analytics are updated automatically. This workflow improves transparency, accountability, and campus safety.
